Deep Learning for Respiratory Disease Classification
from Chest X-Ray Images
Multi-class detection of COVID-19, Pneumonia, and Normal conditions using transfer learning and Grad-CAM explainability
Meera Nallavarambath (25212715)  ·  Anushree Neerulli Sudhakara (24216584)  ·  Supervisor: Dr. Sarp Akçay
1 Motivation
  • Pneumonia causes 2.5M+ deaths annually worldwide
  • COVID-19 and pneumonia appear visually similar on X-rays — challenging even for experts
  • Radiologist shortages in low-resource settings create diagnostic delays
  • AI-assisted screening can reduce misdiagnosis and support clinical decision-making
Clinical goal: Distinguish COVID-19, Pneumonia, and Normal chest X-rays reliably and transparently.
2 Dataset
  • Source: COVIDx-CXR (Figshare)
  • Size: 3,225 chest X-rays
  • Classes: COVID-19 · Normal · Pneumonia
  • Verified by radiologists — minimises label noise
  • Balanced distribution (~1,075 per class)

7 Conclusions
  • Fine-tuned ResNet50 achieved 89% accuracy — 16% above baseline CNN
  • Normal classification nearly perfect at F1 = 0.97
  • COVID AUC = 0.99, Pneumonia AUC = 0.98
  • Grad-CAM confirms model uses clinically meaningful features
  • Fine-tuning layers 3+4 gave 11% improvement over feature extraction
Limitations & Future Work
  • Pneumonia recall: 74% — 26% of cases still missed
  • Small dataset (3,225 images) limits generalisation
  • External validation on hospital data needed
